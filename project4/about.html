Learn about our site!

SceneScreen is a movie recommendation website. The goal of the site is to provide users with quality movie recommendations that fit their individual needs within an appropriate time frame. The site allows users to browse the website by mood, locate the streaming platform the movies are available on, filter by streaming platform, create a movie watchlist, and find movies based on genre. No more scrolling endlessly on streaming sites!
